# Kane Loop — voiceover script

**Target runtime 92s · 213 words · ~2.3 words/sec (measured, unhurried)**

## How to read this

- **Say only the bold lines.** Everything else is direction.
- Timecodes are my *intent*, not a constraint. Record it at a natural pace, then send me the
  timed audio and I will cut the picture to your delivery — not the other way round.
- **Leave the pauses in.** The `—— beat ——` marks are silence I am editing against. They are where
  the picture does the talking. Do not fill them.
- Tone: a senior engineer explaining something they are certain about, to a peer. Level, unhurried,
  slightly dry. No lift at the end of sentences. **No marketing warmth.** If a line sounds like an
  ad, it is being read wrong.
- Every number here is real and checkable in the repo. Say them flatly, like readings off an
  instrument.
